## Find your people in tech ♡

Learning technical skills is important, but finding people who share your interests can make the experience even better.

Rewriting the Code connects university students and early-career professionals through community spaces, mentorship, networking events, and career opportunities.

## What you'll find

- Communities for connecting with other people in tech
- Mentorship and career guidance
- Virtual and in-person events
- Opportunities to connect with employers

## Who should check it out?

College students and early-career professionals looking for a supportive tech community.

## My advice ♡

You don't have to wait until you feel like an expert to start networking. Getting involved while you're still learning is a great way to discover opportunities.
