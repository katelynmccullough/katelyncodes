## Why I recommend it ♡

Codecademy is one of my favorite recommendations for anyone who wants to start learning to code.

What I like about it is that you can actually practice while you're learning instead of just watching someone else write code.

## What you can learn

- Python and programming fundamentals
- SQL and working with databases
- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Data science and machine learning
- Computer science fundamentals

## Who I'd recommend it to

Beginners who want a structured place to start, especially if they learn best by doing.

## A few things to know

Codecademy has a free Basic plan, but some courses, projects, and career paths require a paid subscription.

My suggestion is to explore the free lessons first and see whether you enjoy the format before paying for anything.

## My advice ♡

Don't feel like you need to complete every course before building something.

Learn a concept, practice it, and then try making a tiny project of your own.
