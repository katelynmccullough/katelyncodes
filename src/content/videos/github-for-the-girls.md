## Let's talk about GitHub ♡

GitHub is where you can store your code, track changes, and share your projects.

### What is GitHub?

GitHub is a platform that helps you save, organize, and share your programming projects.

### Why should you use it?

- Build a portfolio of your work.
- Track changes to your projects.
- Collaborate with other developers.
- Show potential employers what you've built.
